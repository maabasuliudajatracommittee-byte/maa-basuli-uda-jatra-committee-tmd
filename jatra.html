Maa Basuli Uda Jatra

Maa Basuli Uda Jatra is an important religious and cultural celebration associated with Maa Basuli at Thakurmunda, Mayurbhanj, Odisha.

The annual celebration brings together devotees, local residents, cultural groups and members of the community through religious observances, cultural programmes and organised festival activities.

Official programme details, notices, events and year-wise Jatra information will be published on this website by the authorised committee.

Important Jatra Information

Annual festival highlights

14 APR

Main Uda Jatra

The main Uda Jatra is observed annually on April 14, coinciding with Odia New Year and Maha Bishuba Sankranti.
