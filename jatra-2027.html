Maa Basuli Uda Jatra – 2027

The annual Maa Basuli Uda Jatra is celebrated at Thakurmunda, Mayurbhanj, Odisha, with religious, traditional and cultural activities.

This page is dedicated to the 2027 Jatra and will contain the official programme, notices, events, gallery updates and other information published by the Maa Basuli Uda Jatra Committee.

Final programme details should be treated as official only when published through the authorised committee notice or announcement.
